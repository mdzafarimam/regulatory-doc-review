CLINICAL STUDY PROTOCOL SYNOPSIS

Protocol Title: A Phase II, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study to
Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of GSK-9921 in Adult Patients with Moderate-to-Severe
Plaque Psoriasis

Protocol Number: GSK-9921-203
Sponsor: GSK Pharmaceuticals
Version: 1.0
Date: 15-Nov-2026

1. BACKGROUND AND RATIONALE
Plaque psoriasis is a chronic immune-mediated skin disease affecting approximately
2-3% of the global population. GSK-9921 is a novel selective IL-23 inhibitor. Preclinical
studies have shown good tolerability in animal models, and Phase I data demonstrated
an acceptable safety profile in healthy volunteers.

2. OBJECTIVES
Primary Objective:
- To evaluate the efficacy of GSK-9921 compared to placebo in achieving PASI-75
  response at Week 16.

Secondary Objectives:
- To evaluate the safety and tolerability of GSK-9921.
- To assess the proportion of patients achieving PASI-90 and PASI-100.
- To evaluate patient-reported quality of life outcomes (DLQI).

3. STUDY DESIGN
This is a multi-center, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group
study. Approximately 240 patients will be randomized in a 2:1 ratio to receive either
GSK-9921 300 mg or matching placebo, administered subcutaneously every 4 weeks for
16 weeks.

4. STUDY POPULATION
Inclusion Criteria:
- Adults aged 18-75 years.
- Diagnosis of moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis for at least 6 months.
- PASI score >= 12 and body surface area (BSA) involvement >= 10% at screening.

Exclusion Criteria:
- History of active tuberculosis or other serious infections within 6 months.
- Prior exposure to any IL-23 or IL-17 inhibitor.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women.

5. TREATMENT AND DOSING
GSK-9921 300 mg or placebo will be administered via subcutaneous injection at Weeks
0, 4, 8, 12, and 16. Dose adjustments are not permitted during the treatment period.

6. EFFICACY ASSESSMENTS
PASI score, BSA, and Investigator Global Assessment (IGA) will be assessed at
Screening, Baseline, and Weeks 4, 8, 12, and 16.

7. SAFETY ASSESSMENTS
Adverse events (AEs) and serious adverse events (SAEs) will be collected throughout
the study. Routine laboratory tests (hematology, chemistry, urinalysis) will be
performed at each visit. Patients will be monitored for signs of infection given the
immunomodulatory mechanism of action.

8. STATISTICAL CONSIDERATIONS
A sample size of 240 patients (160 active, 80 placebo) provides approximately 90%
power to detect a 30% difference in PASI-75 response rate between groups, assuming a
two-sided alpha of 0.05.

9. REFERENCES
1. International Council for Harmonisation (ICH) E6(R2) Good Clinical Practice.
2. FDA Guidance for Industry: Psoriasis - Developing Drugs for Treatment, 2018.
