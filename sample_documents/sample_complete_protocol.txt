CLINICAL STUDY PROTOCOL SYNOPSIS

Protocol Title: A Phase III, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study to
Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of GSK-4410 in Adult Patients with Type 2 Diabetes
Mellitus

Protocol Number: GSK-4410-301
Sponsor: GSK Pharmaceuticals
Version: 2.1
Date: 01-Oct-2026

1. TITLE AND HEADER
See above. This protocol synopsis is intended for IND submission to FDA and CTA
submission to EMA.

2. SCOPE
This protocol defines the design, conduct, and analysis of a confirmatory Phase III
trial of GSK-4410, a once-daily oral GLP-1/GIP dual agonist, in adults with inadequately
controlled Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM). The scope is limited to adult outpatients
across 85 sites in North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific; pediatric and
hospitalized populations are explicitly out of scope and will be addressed in
separate protocols.

3. OBJECTIVES
Primary Objective:
- To evaluate the change from baseline in glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c, a measure of
  average blood glucose over approximately 3 months) at Week 52 for GSK-4410 versus
  placebo.

Secondary Objectives:
- To evaluate the proportion of patients achieving HbA1c < 7.0%.
- To evaluate change from baseline in body weight.
- To characterize the pharmacokinetic (PK) profile of GSK-4410.

4. METHODOLOGY
This is a multi-center, randomized (1:1:1), double-blind, placebo- and
active-controlled, parallel-group study comparing GSK-4410 10 mg, GSK-4410 20 mg,
and placebo, with open-label metformin background therapy. Randomization will be
stratified by baseline HbA1c (<8.5% vs >=8.5%) and use an interactive web response
system (IWRS) with permuted-block randomization (block size of 6). Blinding will be
maintained via matched placebo tablets and a double-dummy design; an independent
unblinded statistician will perform interim analyses. Approximately 1,200 patients
will be enrolled to provide 90% power to detect a 0.5% between-group difference in
HbA1c, assuming a two-sided alpha of 0.05 and 15% dropout.

5. STUDY POPULATION
Inclusion Criteria:
- Adults aged 18-75 years with T2DM diagnosed >= 6 months prior to screening.
- HbA1c between 7.5% and 10.5% at screening, inclusive.
- Body mass index (BMI) between 25 and 45 kg/m^2.
- Stable metformin dose for >= 8 weeks prior to screening.

Exclusion Criteria:
- History of type 1 diabetes mellitus or diabetic ketoacidosis.
- History of pancreatitis, medullary thyroid carcinoma, or Multiple Endocrine
  Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN2).
- Estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) < 30 mL/min/1.73m^2.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women; women of childbearing potential must have a
  negative serum pregnancy test at screening and use protocol-specified
  contraception throughout the study.
- History of malignancy within the past 5 years, except treated basal cell
  carcinoma.

6. TREATMENT AND DOSING
GSK-4410 (10 mg or 20 mg) or matching placebo will be administered orally once
daily, in the morning, with or without food, for 52 weeks. Dose titration from 10 mg
to 20 mg will occur at Week 4 per a pre-specified titration schedule to improve
gastrointestinal tolerability; down-titration is permitted for tolerability per
Section 6.2.

7. EFFICACY ASSESSMENTS
HbA1c, fasting plasma glucose, and body weight will be measured at Screening,
Baseline, and Weeks 4, 12, 24, 36, and 52. Continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) will
be performed in a subset of 200 patients.

8. SAFETY ASSESSMENTS
Adverse events (AEs) and serious adverse events (SAEs) will be collected from
informed consent through 30 days after last dose. Specific solicited AEs for this
drug class (nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, pancreatitis signs/symptoms) will be
actively queried at each visit. Calcitonin levels will be monitored given the
mechanism-related theoretical risk of C-cell hyperplasia observed in rodent
studies (not confirmed in non-human primates or human data to date). A Data Safety
Monitoring Board (DSMB) will review unblinded safety data every 6 months and has
authority to recommend study modification or termination. Stopping rules are
pre-specified in Section 8.4 (e.g., confirmed pancreatitis, calcitonin >
2x ULN confirmed on repeat testing).

9. RISK ASSESSMENT
The principal risks of GSK-4410 are gastrointestinal (nausea, vomiting, diarrhea),
hypoglycemia when combined with insulin secretagogues, and a theoretical risk of
thyroid C-cell tumors based on rodent carcinogenicity data (clinical relevance to
humans not established). Mitigation measures include dose titration, exclusion of
patients with relevant thyroid/pancreatic history, solicited AE queries, and DSMB
oversight. Overall benefit-risk is considered favorable for the target population
based on totality of Phase II data (Protocol GSK-4410-204) showing a 1.8%
placebo-adjusted HbA1c reduction with a manageable GI tolerability profile.

10. STATISTICAL CONSIDERATIONS
The primary analysis will use a mixed model for repeated measures (MMRM) on the
full analysis set (FAS), with multiple imputation as a sensitivity analysis for
missing data. A hierarchical testing procedure will control the family-wise Type I
error rate across the two dose levels and secondary endpoints.
