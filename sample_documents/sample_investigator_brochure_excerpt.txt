Product: GSK-7734 (oral small-molecule JAK1 inhibitor)
Indication: Moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis
Version: 4.0
Date: 03-Mar-2026

BACKGROUND
GSK-7734 works really well and is basically safe for most patients. Our drug is
better than competitor products on the market and has essentially no serious
risks when used as directed. Patients love it.

NONCLINICAL OVERVIEW
Animal studies were conducted. Everything looked fine. No further details are
provided in this excerpt.

CLINICAL EXPERIENCE TO DATE
Phase I and Phase II studies have been completed. The drug was well tolerated.
Efficacy was demonstrated. Full data tables are available upon request but are
not included here.

KNOWN AND POTENTIAL RISKS
JAK inhibitors as a class have been associated with increased risk of serious
infections (including herpes zoster), major adverse cardiovascular events
(MACE), venous thromboembolism (VTE), malignancy, and thrombosis, per FDA boxed
warning requirements for this drug class (see FDA Drug Safety Communication,
2021). This excerpt does not quantify the observed incidence of these events in
GSK-7734 trials, nor does it describe monitoring requirements (e.g., lipid
panels, lymphocyte counts) or dose-adjustment criteria for hepatic/renal
impairment.

GUIDANCE FOR INVESTIGATORS
Investigators should use clinical judgement. Refer questions to the medical
monitor.
