PACKAGE INSERT / PRESCRIBING INFORMATION (DRAFT EXCERPT)

Product Name: Cardiozine (fictional brand) / GSK-5512 (generic substance)
Dosage Form: Oral tablet, 50 mg and 100 mg
Indication: Adjunct therapy for chronic heart failure with reduced ejection
fraction (HFrEF) in adults

1. INDICATIONS AND USAGE
Cardiozine is indicated for the tx of HFrEF pts, NYHA class II-III, to reduce
the risk of CV death and HF hospitalization.

2. DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION
Start at 50mg once a day. May increase to 100mg based on response. Take with or
without food. Renal adjustment: see dosing table (not included in this
excerpt).

3. CONTRAINDICATIONS
- Known hypersensitivity to Cardiozine or its excipients.
- Concurrent use with potent CYP3A4 inhibitors (interaction not further
  described in this excerpt).

4. WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS
- Hyperkalemia: monitor serum potassium.
- Hypotension: use caution in volume-depleted pts.
- Renal function: monitor eGFR periodically; the periodicity and specific
  action thresholds are not specified in this excerpt.

5. ADVERSE REACTIONS
The most commonly reported adverse reactions (incidence >= 5%) in clinical
trials were dizziness, hyperkalemia, and hypotension. Specific incidence rates
versus comparator are not provided in this excerpt.

6. DRUG INTERACTIONS
May interact with other drugs. Consult full prescribing information (not
included) for a complete list.

7. USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS
Pregnancy: Limited data. Lactation: Not recommended. Pediatric use: Safety and
efficacy not established. Geriatric use: No overall differences in safety or
efficacy observed relative to younger patients; this statement is not
supported by referenced subgroup data in this excerpt.

8. CLINICAL STUDIES
Cardiozine was studied in a pivotal Phase III trial (CARDIOZINE-HF-301, n =
4,200) that demonstrated a statistically significant reduction in the composite
endpoint of CV death or HF hospitalization versus placebo (HR not stated in
this excerpt; full results pending publication).
