PERIODIC SAFETY UPDATE REPORT (PSUR) - EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (EXCERPT)

Product: GSK-2290 (monoclonal antibody, anti-IL-5)
Indication: Severe eosinophilic asthma
Reporting Period: 01-Jan-2025 to 31-Dec-2025
Version: 1.0
Date: 20-Feb-2026

1. INTRODUCTION
This PSUR covers the reporting period above for GSK-2290, marketed in 42
countries. Worldwide exposure is estimated at approximately 85,000
patient-years based on sales data.

2. WORLDWIDE MARKETING AUTHORIZATION STATUS
GSK-2290 is approved in the US, EU, Japan, and 39 other countries for severe
eosinophilic asthma as an add-on maintenance treatment.

3. ACTIONS TAKEN FOR SAFETY REASONS
No regulatory actions, label changes, or risk minimization measures were
implemented during this reporting period.

4. CHANGES TO REFERENCE SAFETY INFORMATION
No changes to the Company Core Data Sheet (CCDS) during this period.

5. ESTIMATED EXPOSURE
Approximately 85,000 patient-years of post-marketing exposure, plus 3,200
patients across ongoing and completed clinical trials.

6. PRESENTATION OF INDIVIDUAL CASE SAFETY REPORTS
A total of 410 serious adverse event (SAE) cases were received during the
period, including 18 cases of serious hypersensitivity reactions and 6 fatal
cases assessed as unrelated to study drug by the investigator. Hypersensitivity
reactions, including anaphylaxis, are an identified risk per the Risk
Management Plan (RMP) and are listed in the current product label. No new
unexpected serious adverse reactions were identified in this period.

7. SIGNALS AND EVALUATION
One potential new safety signal (increased incidence of injection-site nodules)
was evaluated and determined not to require label changes based on available
data; continued monitoring was recommended.

8. BENEFIT-RISK ANALYSIS
Based on cumulative safety and efficacy data, the benefit-risk balance for
GSK-2290 in the approved indication remains favorable. No changes to the
benefit-risk conclusion are proposed in this period.

9. CONCLUSION
No new safety concerns were identified that would warrant a change to the
current risk management plan. Routine pharmacovigilance activities will
continue per the RMP.

REFERENCES

2. GSK-2290 Risk Management Plan, v6.0, 2025.

APPROVAL
